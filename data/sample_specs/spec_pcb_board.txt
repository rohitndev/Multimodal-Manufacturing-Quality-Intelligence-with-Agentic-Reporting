PCB ASSEMBLY — PRODUCT SPECIFICATION (PCB-7781)

1. Scope
Acceptance criteria for double-sided 1.6 mm FR-4 PCB panels after SMT reflow.

2. Solder Joints
2.1 Open joints (no solder fillet) are CRITICAL.
2.2 Solder bridges between adjacent pads are CRITICAL.
2.3 Insufficient fillet (<50% pad coverage) is MAJOR.

3. Surface Defects
3.1 Cracks crossing copper traces are CRITICAL.
3.2 Voids in pad area > 25% of pad surface are MAJOR.
3.3 Scratches in silkscreen that do not reach copper are MINOR.

4. Disposition Rules
- CRITICAL defects → FAIL (board scrap).
- MAJOR defects   → QUARANTINE for hand-rework.
- MINOR cosmetic  → PASS but log to MES.
